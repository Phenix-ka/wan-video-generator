## FAQ

**Is it really $1 a video?**
Yes. One credit = one 5-second 720p video, and one credit costs $1. Packs bring the per-video price down to $0.50–$0.80. There is no hidden fee on top — what you pay is the pack price.

**What is Wan 2.2?**
Wan 2.2 is an open-source AI video model (Apache 2.0) that turns images and text into short video clips. This app runs it on a GPU worker and gives you a simple Studio to use it — no setup, no command line, no GPU of your own needed.

**Do I need an account?**
Yes, a free account (email + password) so your videos and credits are saved to you. Signing up gives you 5 free credits, and no payment method is required until you decide to buy more.

**Who owns the videos I generate?**
You do. Wan 2.2 is Apache 2.0 — the model claims no rights over what you generate. You can use your videos commercially, including in ads and client work.

**What about refunds?**
Credits are delivered instantly and are non-refundable once spent on a generated video. If a generation fails on our side (no video delivered), the credit is returned to your balance automatically. If you bought a pack by mistake and haven't used any credits, contact us within 7 days and we'll refund it.
